Questions, answered

What is Caloria Club?

A private wellness club for women, created by fitness influencer Paullina Ruban. Inside: AI meal scanning that reads your plate from one photo, personalized meal & training plans, a 24/7 wellness coach, and a members-only community — all designed around consistency, not restriction.

What does being a Founding Member mean?

You're one of the very first women inside — before public launch. You receive a permanent founder number, the lifetime founding price (it never goes up for you), early access before everyone else, first access to every new feature, and an exclusive Founding Member badge.

Does joining cost anything?

No — joining the Club is completely free. Your founding benefits, including the lifetime founding price, lock in the moment Caloria launches.

When does Caloria launch?

Soon. Founding Members receive exclusive founder updates while it's being built, and a private early-access invitation lands in your inbox before the public doors open.

How do I move up the founders list?

Share the personal invite link from your welcome email (or your founder page). Every friend who joins through it lifts you higher — rankings update instantly.

I joined but can't find my email?

Check Spam or Promotions for “🤍 You're officially a Founding Member.” Your founder page link is inside it, and you can rejoin with the same email any time — we'll recognize you and take you straight to your page.
